## Single-home disciplines (the invariants)

1. **One kind, one home.** No artifact-kind is authored in two places; cross-references
   point, they never copy.
2. **Status is never stored — only queried.** The Climb holds structure; done-ness is
   re-derived from substrate each run (the summit, applied to our own records).
3. **A Ledger precedes a Playbook.** No discipline enters `library/` without a ledger of
   survived cycles (commons/CONTRIBUTING.md).
