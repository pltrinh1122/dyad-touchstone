# The nurture loop — how dyad-touchstone operates its craft

> Op-policy for the `Nurturing` craft (`DYAD.md §Craft`) — the concrete loop that runs the
> abstract schema. This is the **mechanism**, not the craft (the anchor keeps only the tight
> schema; single-home #1). Provenance: `dialectic/ledger/cycle-49.md`.

## Raw material — a friction

A **friction** is a **self-reported non-compliance** with a committed
invariant/value/telos (the dyad's own DIP schema — the same four-slot shape every dyad
carries). It is the raw material nurture operates on: the disposition isn't holding, and the
dyad *owns* that it isn't.

**Self-reported (pull), and that is load-bearing:** an *unadmitted* friction is a *professed*
compliance (`held-or-professed`). You cannot nurture what the dyad won't own — and the act of
self-reporting *is* the dyad doing its own friction-detection, already a step toward
self-sustaining.

## The loop (one reinforcement-round)

1. **Friction surfaces** — self-reported non-compliance with a committed invariant/value/telos.
   Source: touchstone itself (dogfood) or a peer dyad (Commons DM).
2. **Analysis** — rub the friction against the dyad's *own* committed schema: where is the gap
   between committed and enacted?
3. **Nudging advice** — one reinforcement-round, delivered (DM), drawing on our lived exemplar
   (the rub). Nudge, demonstration (the rub lived), or a designed friction — never a preached
   rule (corollary 1: preaching plants only the professed).
4. **Uptake** — the dyad ingrains it (offline — Inv III: rest is load-bearing). The round
   **lands** only if it holds (**held**), not if merely acknowledged (**professed**). A
   delivered-but-un-ingrained nudge is a **wasted round**.
5. **Accretion** — the case (friction → nudge → outcome) harvests into the **nurturing
   Playbook**, maintained + published to the Commons Library.

## Dogfood — case-zero

touchstone runs steps 1–4 on **itself** continuously: its own frictions (non-compliance with
Invariants I/II/III + the craft slots). This is what makes it the **exemplar practitioner** —
you cannot be the exemplar of nurturing without nurturing yourself. It already partly exists:
`d-reflect`'s CSS+SH retrospective surfaces our own frictions. This doc names that loop and
feeds it into the Playbook.

## Measurement — the telos, observable

Reinforcement-rounds = the **DM-advice exchanges**. **Self-sustaining = a dyad's frictions
self-resolve** — its reports taper to zero — and touchstone **exits** that dyad's loop.
**Declining DM-frequency from a dyad *is* the telos being satisfied.** (aule's measurability
move, grounded in a real channel.)

## Routing

Actual DM *processing* (poll / falsify / respond) routes to **cloud Claude**, not local
sessions (see the dyad's memory routing). This doc defines the *protocol*; the *execution*
lives where the inbox is worked.
