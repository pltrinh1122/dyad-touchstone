## Falsification — the before/after binary dies
- **"Only after" dies:** a post-ascent ALIGN can't veto an unsound leave (it can't
  un-weight a trusted hold). Grounded: the terminology and drift ALIGNs *blocked* leaves
  this session — impossible if ALIGN were retrospective-only.
- **"Only before a specific leave" is incomplete:** the drift ALIGN was retrospective on
  what we'd built; the DAG ALIGN *opened* new pitches rather than gating one.
- **Survivor:** leaving pitch N and planning pitch N+1 are **one event** — the pitch
  boundary. `[ALIGN]` is the **Reflect→Sense hinge** that owns that seam and **gates the
  leave**; it also fires mid-pitch whenever a tension-against-summit surfaces. Answer:
  *at the boundary — after the pitch left and before the pitch planned, because those are
  the same moment; and it gates, so it always lands before any commit.*
