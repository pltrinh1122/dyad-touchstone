# Ledger — cycle #6: borrowed channel-tags falsified; a lived wu-wei marker lexicon homed

## Date
2026-06-03

## The claim under test
Can the inherited channel tags (`[ALIGN]`, `[IDEATE]`, generic "gate replies") be
replaced by a **lived**, self-derived intent-marker lexicon — single-token markers the
Operator leads with to disambiguate intent at low cognitive/token load (the "Graffiti"
move: minimal strokes optimized for the recognizer, because natural language is
inefficient)? And: is the **interface plane** (how the dyad cycles) actually separable
from the **ascent plane** (moves on the climb)?

## Falsification — what died, what held
- **Clean interface/summit separation — partially dies, survives reshaped.** `[ALIGN]` is
  summit-welded (it gates the leave *against the summit*) — it cannot be filed as pure
  interface. Survivor: the seam is real (sanctioned by single-home discipline #5), but some
  markers are hybrids; the cut is interface · summit · weld, not a clean two-way split.
- **"Coin fresh terms" dies.** Discard-then-recoin is max-force. Survivor: reuse what rings
  sound (`rub`, `read`), coin only the gaps — min force. Lived mismatch is the falsifier,
  not novelty.
- **Noun-metaphor markers die.** `gate` didn't fire as an imperative; `anchor` collided
  with the Anchor artifact-kind. Survivor: the marker must *be the verb* — driven by the
  climbing question "what do you DO at a pin/anchor?" → **`clip`**.
- **Decorative punctuation dies.** `clip!` (for symmetry with `lean!`) refuted: `!` on
  `lean` is *contrastive* (disambiguates `lean?`/`lean!`); `clip` has no `clip?`, so `!`
  unlocks nothing, and `clip` takes a payload while the `lean` toggles don't. Plane is
  encoded in the table, not punctuation.
- **Silence-as-verdict dies.** Default is **not-clipped** (no lock without the gate; mirrors
  the touchstone). Three states: clipped · open (*un-refuted, not verified*) · refuted.
  Silence holds a candidate *open* — never inferred as yes or no.

## Survivor — homed into the Anchor (D4)
Lexicon: **`read:`** (orient) · **`rub:`** (attack) · **`riff:`** (lean, no gate) ·
**`rack:`** (carry/hold) · **`fb:`** (adjust behavior, don't contest) · **`lean?`**
(read, hold) · **`lean!`** (read + take move) · **`clip`** (render + lock).
Syntax `marker: payload` (colon — matches `key: value`, never line-start prose); `lean?`/
`lean!` self-delimiting. Supersedes `[IDEATE]` (→`riff:`); keeps `[ALIGN]` (marker still
*on the rack*) and the gate-reply vocabulary. Design rationale parked in agent-memory, not
the Anchor (keeps it loadable).
