## The principle (handle: `without-you`)
> **A pitch leaves the substrate so the next run can re-derive the truth without the
> departing leader** — present **Fresh** (caches re-derived), past **Frozen** (records never
> forged), open **Flagged** (uncertainty preserved, never shown as settled).

The 3-F verifier is the rub: each F guards one stratum against one lie —
*Fresh* kills the present-tense lie (stale cache), *Frozen* the past-tense lie (forged
record), *Flagged* the modal lie (open-ness hidden as resolved). One question runs all
three: *could a hostile reader, given only the substrate and none of your memory, be misled?*

It is the **summit's operational face**: the epistemic summit says *don't assert what you
can't defend*; `without-you` says *don't leave what can't be re-derived* — same rope, two
ends. "Without you" means without your **memory**, not without your **deposits**: a `pin:` is
a piton left *for* the next run. The substrate carries your pitons, never your head.

## Why `without-you` won the handle
`bomber` (climbing slang — a placement trusted absolutely without the one who placed it;
register-perfect) and `no-narrator` were the live alternatives. Operator took the
plain-descriptive `without-you`: zero translation, self-explaining, names the test directly.

## The rub-kind axis (surfaced, not yet formalized)
The chain exposed that `pin:` discriminates on **one** axis only — *fiat vs claim*
(authority / falsifiability). It does **not** discriminate **how** a thing is rubbed:

| Type | rub-kind | the move |
|---|---|---|
| fact | **observe** | query the substrate this run (`file_exists`/`contains`) |
| principle | **apply** | run it on cases; check verdicts + coherence with summit |
| theory | **predict** | force a novel prediction, test it |
| (fiat) | **none** | unfalsifiable preference — authority-only gate |

The Touchstone (`playbook.py`) implements **observe only**. So today it can soundly re-rub
**facts** but not **principles/theories** — pin one of those and you get a sacred cache
(violates #2 status-never-stored, #4 un-refuted-is-not-verified). `follow:` is the **apply-rub
made runnable**: following a pitch *cleans* it, so `follow: <principle>` = **apply ∧ re-rub
in one move** — the missing mechanism that lets a non-fact pin stay falsifiable.

## What is clipped vs what stays OPEN
- **Clipped (locked):** the *handle* `without-you`, and `follow:` as a marker. These are
  naming fiat — unilateral.
- **OPEN (un-refuted, NOT verified):** the principle's **soundness as load-bearing**. It has
  **not** been apply-rubbed across real pitches — only born in a riff and applied once
  (to the dag.yaml-name case, this session). Per #4, *pinned ≠ sacred*: the name is locked,
  the claim is not. First real use of `follow: without-you` on a pitch IS its first
  apply-rub; it earns Ledger proofs there, and only a Ledger of proofs precedes a Playbook
  (#3). Formalizing the rub-kind axis into the Pin-protocol stays **on the rack** until a
  live pin forces it (#5 — don't elaborate the apparatus to admire it).
