## What was clipped
The Operator hat model (AGENT.md D4) is **not** three co-equal seats. It is **two Operator roles,
keyed by the object of disposal** — same human, role set by *what is being disposed*, not by person:

- **{TO} Touchstone Operator** (aka *Operator*, the default) — disposes the dyad's own **climb**
  (steer · select frontier · ratify · gate merges); the seat the session-loop runs on.
- **{FO} Founding Operator** — disposes the **Commons artifact** (authority at the Founding gate #5).
  Activates *in-session here* when this dyad owns a shared-infra asset (e.g. a state-machine engine).

A single session can wear **both** (this one did — see below).

## Why it matters (SPAOR impact — the Operator's stated stake)

which makes SPAOR's **Observe look optional** → a candidate trusted because nobody rubbed it =
stale-cache at the governance layer, against the summit ("verified, not hoped"). Retyping
falsification as the *always-run Observe function* (agent + Touchstone) makes the V-check structural,
not occasional. Plan-selection is now **two-valued** ({TO} climb vs {FO} Commons).
