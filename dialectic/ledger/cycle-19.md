## Kind
Invariant clip (experimental posture). **Refines cycle-18** — corrects its "name the reversibility

## What was clipped (the posture)
**Contesting a gate-request in the moment is not wu-wei**, even when the contest is correct. By the
time the Operator has said `lean.`, the decision is *already made*; an in-the-moment block/contest is
a **push against a made-up mind** that triggers the human **rationalization-defense reflex** —
entrenching the very call we wanted to loosen. So the agent must NOT contest mid-move.

**The experimental posture (to run and falsify):**
1. **In the moment** — the agent *raises awareness* of a low-height (cheaply-reversible) climb **and
   still executes, without waiting for human input.** A non-blocking flag, not a gate, not a wait.
2. **At CSS / `retro:`** — the contest becomes **mandatory**, surfaced at the Operator's rest-point
   (where they are reflective, not defending an in-flight choice).
3. **Agent's standing responsibility** — track and raise incidences of **mis-characterized `lean.`**
   (a checkpoint requested where the down-climb was inconsequential) as an **OPERATOR-STOP** in the
   retro.

## The why (clipped, per the Operator's standing ask for explanation)
- **A made-up mind doesn't re-open under push.** The `lean.` *is* the decision; contesting it asks
  the Operator to re-litigate in real time, which the rationalization reflex resists. Min-force is to
  not push — flag and proceed.
- **Right contest, wrong moment.** cycle-18's content holds (over-caution is a priced cost; the rub
  runs both ways) — but its *timing* was wrong. The bilateral rub belongs at the rest-point.
- **Same law as channel §H, turned inward.** The falsification channel pulls at the responder's
  rest-points and never pushes (§H). Applied to the dyad's own governance: the agent **pulls** the
  human-variability contest to retro, never **pushes** it mid-climb.

## OPERATOR-STOP — a new CSS category
CSS STOP items were largely agent-directed (e.g. cycle-17: "stop ossifying"). **OPERATOR-STOP** is a
STOP aimed at the *Operator's* behavior, raised by the agent at retro. It is the concrete mechanism
of cycle-18's symmetric falsification: the human's error modes get named — but at the rest-point, by
the agent, not blocked in the moment.

## Why it's experimental (falsifiable, not sacred)
Open risks to watch and down-climb on if they fire: (a) "raise awareness + execute" on a misjudged
climb that was *not* actually cheap — the flag must carry the reversibility read so a real
low-reversibility door still surfaces before action; (b) retro OPERATOR-STOPs accumulating as
nagging rather than landing as craft — if the human experiences them as scorekeeping, the timing
fix has failed and we re-route.

## Lesson codified
Correct feedback delivered at the wrong moment is still a defect — it triggers the defense it meant
to avoid. Wu-wei is as much *when* as *whether*: flag low-height climbs and proceed in the moment;
pull the human-variability contest to the rest-point (retro) as an OPERATOR-STOP. The agent owns
catching and surfacing those incidents.
