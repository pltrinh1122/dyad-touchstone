## What was clipped (the OPERATOR-STOP)
**The Operator commits to stop exercising `lean!` without a clear mental model of the intended
outcome.** A `lean!` is the *delegated gate* — full send, no checkpoint. Firing it with no model of
what the move should produce delegates a move the Operator has not actually grounded — which
manufactures moves *neither* party has modeled.

## The why (clipped, per standing ask)
- **A delegated gate presupposes the delegator modeled the outcome.** `lean!` says "take this move, I
  trust the read." But trust delegated over an *un-modeled* outcome isn't trust — it's **abdication**:
  nobody is holding the intended end. The mental model is the **precondition** for `lean!`, not an
  optional extra.
- **The under-caution mirror of #18.** cycle-18 named the human's *over*-caution (a `lean.` from
  control-need). This is the inverse failure: *under*-caution — full delegation on thin ground. Both
  are human-variability error modes the touchstone now guards (cycle-18 symmetric falsification).
- **The down-climb was the tell, not the cure.** Lived this session: the opening `lean!` on
  `gap_rings_sound` was fired with no mental model and blind trust → a pitch that manufactured the
  `gap-rederive` disposition + `gap-failloud` revision → Commons PR #50 → retracted (cycles 16-19).
  The cheap down-climb *recovered* it, but recovery is not a substitute for a model up front.

## Lesson codified
`lean!` is delegation, and delegation without a modeled outcome is abdication, not trust. The
precondition for a full-send gate is a clear mental model of the intended end — held by *someone*. Its
absence is a falsifiable Operator error, surfaced at the rest-point and clipped here as the dyad's
first OPERATOR-STOP.
