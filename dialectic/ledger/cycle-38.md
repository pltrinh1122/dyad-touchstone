## The claim (clipped, named `cup-or-stove`)
**Set intervention-force to the leftover-after-cleanup, and to nothing else.** The magnitude of any
intervention — gate, help, correction, control, immutability-barrier, in-moment contest — is computed
from a single variable: **residual un-self-recoverability** = the cost left after the system's own best
recovery. Default is minimum force, because a *self-recoverable* failure is not a cost to prevent but a
**rep to harvest**. Raise force above the floor *only* to the degree the failure can't self-recover.

**The mnemonic (the accessible handle, `clip`'d this cycle):** *cup or stove?* — a toddler about to
spill a **cup** (wipes up, ~zero leftover → let it run, zero force; the spill is how they learn) vs. about
to grab a **stove** (burns, real leftover → grab the hand, full force). Don't read the drama; read the
leftover: **wipe or burn?** Invoked as `follow: cup-or-stove`. It is a *principle-handle*, **not** a new
channel marker — the channel lexicon stays closed (adding a token there is the cycle-20/24 over-add).

## Why it rings sound — n=4 across unrelated planes
The two seed-pins aren't analogous; they're the **same law** on different substrates. "Blindness" is just
the learning-plane name for "can't self-recover." Once the variable is named, four pins fall in as children
of one parent:
1. **World-act** — `gate-height ← reversibility` (htil-before-external-publish). Leftover = can't undo the act.
2. **Learning-loop** — `help-height ← blindness` (parenting invariants). Leftover = the foreclosed loop.
3. **Substrate** — `gate-at-create-not-write ← the sha` (nothing-immutable-but-the-sha). *create* is irreversible, *write* isn't.
4. **Dialectic** — `contest-now-vs-defer ← marker convergence` (re-alignment-protocol). A forming model is cheap to redirect; a made-up mind contested in-moment triggers rationalization-defense.
Four planes — world, mind, substrate, dialectic — one force-setting rule. The cross-plane recurrence is
why the gut fired.

## The rub that survived (and refined the variable)
Attack: *reversible-but-catastrophic* — a failure you can undo but each instance is brutally costly; binary
"reversibility" wrongly says don't gate it. Resolution: the variable is **residual unrecoverable cost**
(cost left after best recovery), not binary reversibility. Irreversibility is the limiting case (leftover =
full cost); blindness is the loop-case (leftover = the dead loop); reversible-but-costly has leftover = the
recovery toll. Force scales with that. Stronger than either original pin; absorbs the counterexample.
