  deterministic validation.
- **G→V was clipped refuted-as-telos** (`cycle-43.md:67-71`): *"G→V is substrate
  table-stakes (every agent; rising into native G+V), so anchoring a craft to it
  regresses into ground the substrate is flooding — anti-wu-wei."*
- **`craft_invariant` (anchor):** *"don't regress into the substrate; never rebuild
  the capability the substrate is absorbing."* Annexing GAP as our shared
  contribution **is** that regress.
- **Single-home #5 pre-flagged this node:** *"Summit-climb is method, not summit… an
  apparatus that only tests itself has drifted. (Flag: summit-climb may be a
  form-level scaffold to propose at the Founding gate — not annexed as our summit.)"*
- **Stale, not wrong-at-birth:** the FR was submitted `2026-06-03` and
  `gap_live_external` discharged (PR#61) `2026-06-08` — both **before** the cycle-43
  reforge. `cycle-43.md:108` names the GAP framing as *"under the **old**
  `craft_telos` (G→V)."* The node is progress toward a destination the summit no
  longer points at.
- **Independent second break:** `strengthened` needs an *independent* SURVIVED

  NEEDS-SCOPING from a 2/3-shared-axis sibling (dyad-steward), and no frontier

   toward annexing it as our summit*, not that the claim is false.
