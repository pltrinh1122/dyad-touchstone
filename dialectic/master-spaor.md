## Definition

**The master SPAOR is the dyad's *session-scoped* loop — born at Stand Up, dead at Stand Down.**
It runs the five phases once at session scale, spawning nested SPAOR jobs inside its Act.

| Phase | IS the ritual | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Sense** | **Stand Up** | load the Anchor; re-derive the frontier via the Touchstone (never cache); pull the inbox; **commit the session-target** |
| **Plan** | — | select from the computed frontier — *the Operator's seat; the falsifier refuses to select* |
| **Act** | — (the body) | spawn jobs concurrently — pitches & playbooks; **[ALIGN]** gates pitch-boundary leaves |
| **Observe** | — | join the jobs' results; validate each against its `done_when` |
| **Reflect** | **Stand Down** | fired by **`d-reflect`**, **one act, two outputs** — *harvest* (survived proofs → Ledger: the **work**) **and** the **CSS+OR** Continue/Start/Stop pass → the **craft**: how we climbed; verify clean tree + Fresh/Frozen/Flagged; confirm the Climb holds in-flight work; end |

**Intermission** = an intra-session **Reflect→Sense** re-entry (segment seam; resume the same climb).

## The end-in-mind law (the completing piece)

**Stand Up is not complete until Plan commits a session-target — the pitch this session tops out —
whose `done_when` IS the Stand-Down trigger.** No committed target → no computable session-done →
the loop spawns without terminus (the failure this very session demonstrated live, cycle-13).

- The target is **intent — the Operator's seat** (the falsifier refuses to select). The agent
  *surfaces the question at Sense and holds*; it never invents the target.
- Even an exploratory/riff session needs a **terminus** — a stopping condition (a budget, a
  decision-point, "one clip-worthy candidate"), not necessarily a deliverable. The failure mode is
  *no declared terminus*, never exploration itself.

## Act spawns jobs — fractal SPAOR

`Act` is a **spawner**, not a primitive. Each spawned job is itself a SPAOR, and jobs may spawn jobs.

- **Pitch** = a Climb node opened into a **bespoke** SPAOR (Plan designed on the fly).
- **Playbook** = a **crystallized** SPAOR — `follow: <playbook>` spawns a proven, pre-planned job
  (apply ∧ re-rub). `live → write → share` IS the crystallization of a repeatedly-lived bespoke
  SPAOR into a spawnable one; "writing the playbook" stores the *method* (re-validated each
  `follow:`), as the DAG stores structure and re-derives status.
- **Act = fan-out, Observe = fan-in.** The frontier is a set, so Act spawns concurrent jobs; Observe
  is the join/barrier. Single-home (#1) is the lock-free enabler (disjoint homes, no collisions);
  re-derive-each-run is the race-free read.

## Mortality + continuity

The loop is **mortal** — there is no standing process. Between sessions nothing runs. Continuity
lives entirely in the **substrate** (Anchor · Climb · Ledger): Stand Down **flushes** state to it
(Fresh/Frozen/Flagged), Stand Up **reconstitutes** by re-deriving. So **`without-you` is the
inter-session handoff law**, and the **supra-session spine is the Climb** — climbed by a series of
mortal session-loops. Scale stack: **pitch ⊂ segment ⊂ session (= master loop) ⊂ Climb.**
