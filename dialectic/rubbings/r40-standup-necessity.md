# Rubbing `r40-standup-necessity` — Stand-Up session-target: load-bearing or ceremony?

Opened 2026-06-09 (post cycle-39), from the retro rub-back. Scope: the **session-target /
end-in-mind** half of Stand Up only — the branch-confirm half is split off to a CSI-guard
(mechanization, not a ritual question).

## Claim under test

The Stand-Up **session-target** (commit the pitch this session tops out; cycle-13 "end-in-mind
law") is **load-bearing** — a session without one loses the thread / spawns forever.
*Falsifier stance:* it is **ceremony** — sessions self-terminate at natural value without a
committed target, and the cost of the ritual exceeds any harm its absence causes.

## Why it's open (the confound)

n≈1 each way, unresolved. The anchor asserts necessity (cycle-13) — but *un-refuted is not
verified* (#4), and the claim is now under active attack from two directions: a targetless
session that still produced durable infra, and a peer dyad that dropped the formal ritual. One
session can't tell load-bearing from ceremony; only telemetry across sessions can.

## Falsification method (unprimed — load-bearing)

Per assessing session, the observer records, at Stand Down: (a) was a session-target committed?
(b) did the session reach natural value or **sprawl / lose the thread**? (c) did any concrete
harm **trace to the absence** (un-gated snowball, work on the wrong branch, no terminus)?
(d) rough ritual cost. The discriminator: *harm-traces-to-absence* (→ necessary) vs *no-harm,
cost-paid* (→ ceremony). Do not let a single productive targetless session settle it — sprawl
may be intermittent.

## Resolution criterion

After N ≥ ~5 sampled sessions: if harm **never** traces to the absence and cost > benefit →
**refute** (ceremony; drop the formal target). If absence **repeatedly** causes lost-thread /
sprawl / ungoverned work → **strengthen** (keep, possibly mechanize). Harvest the verdict to the
Ledger as a Proof.
